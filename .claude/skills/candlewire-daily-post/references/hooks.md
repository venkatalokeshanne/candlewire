# Hook playbook (cover headline + caption line 1). All patterns must stay true to the data.
1. Number + tension: "S&P 500 -4.4%: WORST DAY SINCE APRIL 2025"
2. Contrast: "NASDAQ +1.09% BUT THE WEEK ENDS IN THE RED"
3. Superlative with a date: "BIGGEST ONE-DAY DROP IN 19 MONTHS"
4. Unfinished fact: "CHIPS WON BACK ONLY A QUARTER OF THURSDAY'S LOSS" (the cover number answers part, the swipe answers why)
5. Calendar stakes: "CPI TUESDAY: WHAT THE MARKET IS PRICING"
6. Head-to-head: ACTUAL vs EXPECTED, LEADER vs LAGGARD, MORNING vs NOW (a `versus` slide).
7. Winner vs loser: "INTEL +7.7% WHILE BROADCOM BARELY MOVED"
Never: invented drama, hidden caveats, a headline the numbers do not support, "you won't believe", like/comment/tag bait.
Interactive elements that are allowed: versus and chain slides, a genuine question in the caption, a Story poll made from the post (see `story` in data.json).
Slide-writing: one idea per slide; end each slide with a reason to swipe (the `nx` teaser); make one slide worth screenshotting (stat or board).
