# Trend scan (max 3 calls, before research)
1. Stocktwits connector (if present): trending symbols (limit 10), then sentiment for the top 2 tickers. Say "Stocktwits users" when you cite it. It tells you what retail is talking about, not what is true.
2. One web search: today's biggest market story or most talked-about moment (include the date).
3. `inspiration/notes.md` if it exists.
Output: ONE angle, ONE hook (see hooks.md), and whether a mascot meme slide fits today's mood. Write them in one line in your notes, then move on.
Not allowed: scraping Instagram, X or TikTok; copying text or images; reposting other people's memes; fabricating quotes or screenshots of real people.
